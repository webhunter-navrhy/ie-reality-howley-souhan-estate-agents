About the property

Description property

Howley Souhan Estate Agents are delighted to present this lovely three-bedroom detached bungalow extending to approximately 113 sq.m. and occupying an elevated site in a quiet cul-de-sac surrounded by similar type properties. The property's elevated position is undoubtedly one of its most appealing features, with stunning panoramic westerly facing views across Blessington Lakes and the surrounding Wicklow countryside.

The accommodation is well laid out and comprises an entrance hall, living room, kitchen, utility room, three generous double bedrooms, en-suite shower room and family bathroom. The property provides a comfortable home with excellent potential for a purchaser to modernise and make it their own.

The bungalow sits on a sloping site, with gardens extending around the property. While the gardens would benefit from some attention and landscaping, they offer considerable potential and could be transformed into a wonderful country garden, taking full advantage of the elevated setting and magnificent views. For the keen gardener, this is a genuine opportunity to create something special.

The property is ideally positioned for those seeking a peaceful rural setting while remaining within easy reach of local amenities. Ballyknockan Village is renowned for its attractive stone-built character and its location at the foothills of the Wicklow Mountains. Blessington is approximately 10 - 12 minutes' drive away, providing a good range of shops, cafés, schools, restaurants and everyday amenities.

For commuters, Dublin City Centre is approximately 40km away, with access to Dublin via the N81 and M50, making the property an appealing option for those looking to enjoy a quieter countryside lifestyle within commuting distance of the capital.

The combination of its elevated position, breathtaking lake views, generous site, quiet cul-de-sac setting and proximity to Blessington and Dublin makes this a property with considerable appeal and potential.
